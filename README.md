# PulsePlan
Hi, I'm Rishav, the 15y/o founder of "PulsePlan" from India. This is my first published app. The idea is simple - Make PulsePlan an everyday health app for those who care about their health. PulsePlan isn't just a normal health app which does all of it, from keeping track of schedule and steps to analyzing your health and making up of routine PERSONALISED FOR YOU. 
PulsePlan is your daily health OS.
→ Calories, water, sleep & steps — all tracked | Workout planner with exercise videos | Indian food library + barcode scan | AI that reads your health reports and PDFs | Builds your personal routine. Automatically.
